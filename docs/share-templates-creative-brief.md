# FOND SHARE TEMPLATES — CREATIVE DIRECTOR'S DECK

> **Author:** Creative Director, Fond
> **Date:** July 2026
> **Status:** Internal — Not for distribution

---

## Creative Mandate

The goal is NOT to make beautiful templates.

The goal is to make people stop scrolling and ask:

**"What app is THAT?"** or **"I NEED this."**

Every share should feel like a cultural artifact rather than an app screenshot.

### References
Spotify Wrapped · Apple marketing · Duolingo social · BeReal · Arc Browser · Nothing · Linear · Netflix marketing · Formula 1 graphics · Vogue · A24 · MSCHF

### Avoid
Certificates · generic report cards · boring score cards · plain leaderboards · obvious gradients · "Top 1%" cards · Canva vibes · startup-trying-to-be-cool energy

### Target Qualities
Unexpected · premium · emotionally charged · internet-native · memeable · luxurious · instantly recognizable · collectible · worthy of being saved · worthy of being reposted
