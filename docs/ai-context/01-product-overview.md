# Product Overview: Fond

## What It Is

Fond (formerly "LoveBoard") is a **gamified social platform where couples compete via AI-scored romantic storytelling**. Users post stories about their partner's romantic gestures, an AI judge scores them out of 100, and couples are ranked on local, city, and global leaderboards.

**Tagline:** "Your Relationship Has a Score."
**Secondary tagline:** "Post one story. AI judges it. Compete with couples worldwide."

## Why It Exists

Fond turns private romantic moments into a public, competitive, entertainment product. It sits at the intersection of:
- **Social media** (storytelling + feed + likes/comments)
- **Gamification** (scores, leaderboards, tiers, streaks)
- **AI entertainment** (AI persona that roasts/praises users with personality)
- **Dating culture** (couples showing off, relationship flexing)

## Target Users

| Persona | Description | Needs | Behaviors |
|---------|-------------|-------|-----------|
| **The Show-Off Couple** | Young couples (20-35) who love posting about their relationship | Validation, competition, social proof | Posts weekly, checks leaderboard, shares verdict cards |
| **The Competitive Partner** | One half of a couple who wants to "win" at romance | Gamification, scores, ranking | Posts daily, optimizes stories for AI score, recruits partner |
| **The Lurker** | Single or coupled users who watch but don't post | Entertainment, voyeurism, inspiration | Scrolling feed, leaderboard browsing, anonymous confessions |
| **The Anonymous Confessor** | Users who want drama/anonymity | Venting, anonymity, reactions | Uses anonymous mode, posts confessions, reacts to others |

## Core Business Goals

1. **User acquisition** — Viral share cards spread via social media
2. **Engagement** — Daily posts, feed browsing, leaderboard checking
3. **Monetization** — Freemium model (₹299/mo or ₹2,499/yr) for unlimited posts, partners, editing
4. **Network effects** — Circles/bonds create group dynamics, invite codes drive growth

## Core Value Propositions

1. **AI-as-entertainment** — Not a utility AI ("how romantic is this?") but a personality AI with sass, roasts, and dramatic verdicts
2. **Gamified romance** — Turns relationship maintenance into a competitive sport
3. **Social proof** — Leaderboards provide public validation for private gestures
4. **Anonymous outlet** — Confession mode for relationship venting without identity

## Core Workflows

1. **Sign up → Onboard → Find partner → Post story → Get scored → See rank → Share**
2. **Browse feed → Like/Comment → Click profile → Connect → Invite to circle**
3. **Toggle anonymous → Post confession → React to confessions → Browse**

## Key Metrics (Inferred from Product Design)

- Posts per user per day (2/day free cap → engagement ceiling)
- Leaderboard rank (gamification driver)
- Streak length (retention mechanism)
- Share card creations (virality)
- Premium conversion (revenue)
- Circle invites (growth)
